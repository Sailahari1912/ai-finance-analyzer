# 💳 AI Finance & Spending Analyzer

An intelligent personal finance and spending analyzer powered by Streamlit, Plotly, and Google Gemini GenAI. Supports universal statement parsing (CSV, Excel, and password-protected PDFs), automated merchant normalization, category tracking, budget alerts, and an interactive AI financial advisor.

---

### 1. Prerequisites
Make sure you have **Python 3.10+** and **Git** installed on your computer.
